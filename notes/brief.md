---
title: "The leak is not the slick"
subtitle: "Sentinel-1 misses three NOSDRA-confirmed swamp spills in the western Niger Delta"
author: Wisdom Akpabio
date: September 2026
---

Three oil leaks in the western Niger Delta are on the public NOSDRA books. Sentinel-1 does not put a dark slick on any of the three Joint Investigation Visit pins.

Heritage logged 700 barrels on the Trans-Forcados pipeline at Odidi-Kantu (20 February 2026). Renaissance logged 2,466 barrels on the Trans-Ramos at Odimodi (21 April 2026) and 1,322 barrels at Well 11L Opukushi (26 December 2025). Odimodi and Opukushi carry JIV spill areas of 0.07 km² and 0.03 km². The habitat is swamp, not open sea.

The same-look Sentinel-1 pair after each incident still scores **0 hits out of 3**. A hit means the JIV pin sits inside a detected dark patch, or within 500 metres of one. Every pin sits on land or swamp, 41 m, 266 m and 184 m from the nearest creek. Odidi is a bright return (−2.3 dB), not oil. A marine rule that oil is darker than −22 dB finds almost no pixels. Water in these creeks already sits near −16 to −18 dB.

Odidi is the near miss: the closest darkening is 570 m from the pin, just outside the 500 m rule. The whole local creek also darkened by about 2 dB, so wind and oil are not separated. Opukushi’s only dark patch was already dark before the spill. None of the detector square kilometres is a measured slick.

The story is the miss. A recipe written for offshore films does not confirm a swamp leak at the point NOSDRA visited.
